# RC197 Cosmic automatic refuge follows the actual beam

Follow-up to RC196 ray change: native auto-combat refuge still computed signed distance from y=x and nominated old square corners. It now computes the actual line's perpendicular normal, proposes both nearby exits from that band, and considers the current player-side polygon. Final candidates are projected into the legal player-side radius and checked against the native impact predicate after terrain sampling. This affects the already-enabled automatic movement intent only. Manual movement/blink, immunity, damage, HP and warning duration are unchanged; it does not grant automatic defensive effects.

Twenty-five oblique beam/player cases execute actual native Di/refuge plus actual arena/polygon; nearest safe legal goals are within5world units. dt terrain and optional collision hooks are fixture dependencies. Before test fails, after passes. Inherits RC193–196: right Cosmic mask/body, player-facing retreat, serial scatter, post-bounce flight/bitmap heading, blink and actual +23.959%floor,14 story narrow-volley styles. No public deployment; main remains4f08. Requires candidate visual/device/natural-progression review before release.
