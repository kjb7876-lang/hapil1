boss alone reached its first lethal hit. This did not satisfy the approved
requirement that either combatant's first lethal hit starts the saved duel.

or simultaneous lethal contact. Each actor at zero HP is restored to 22%
maximum HP. A living opponent retains its HP and healing ceiling, including
already inflicted boss damage. Both awaken for seven combat seconds and the
arena becomes the opposed black/white and black/red halves. The saved clash
marker consumes the normal encounter revival and a pending EGO seventh entry.
A later lethal contact cannot grant another revival. Boss defeat after the
clash completes the encounter; player defeat follows the ordinary death path.
