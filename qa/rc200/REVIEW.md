Minimal changes:
1. Actual final RC138 ii entry wrapper calls layout.resetCamera(s) after the existing entry call. resetCamera deletes only WeakMap camera, framing and actor-size caches. No save field or serial counter is added, and native release→entry→enforce order and return value remain. The hook works for same-object/same-zone entry without requiring a clock change. This is a test of the real wrapper with a fixture map loader, not an actual map traversal.
2. Finite simulation-time rollback resets the same caches even without a map call or zone/window change. Equal-time duplicate calls do not reset; absent/nonfinite time does not manufacture a rollback.
3. Each whole/hero/boss channel retains the union of essential framing rectangles seen during the current epoch. A newly larger body/raised weapon can cause the camera to widen to contain it; smaller/removed bodies never narrow that union or zoom back in. Projectiles are excluded by the existing framing policy. Real window, orientation, safe-area or scene changes still select a fresh frame and may refit in either direction. Original epoch viewport is retained during render/backing jitter and one-way expansion.

This explicitly relaxes the previous 'only resize can change zoom' claim: newly larger essential presentation is a necessary safety exception. It prevents repeated large↔small zoom oscillation rather than promising zero scale changes while unknown larger art arrives. Multiple genuinely larger extents can produce multiple one-way expansions; none can trigger zoom-in before a reset. Normal resize handling, expanded floor, right boss band, separate portrait channels and existing rotation guide are retained. No body movement, damage, narrative, save, image bytes or hit geometry changes.
